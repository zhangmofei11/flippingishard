Level Walkthrough

Master every level and guide Flippy back to his owner

1 Chapter 1: First Flips

1-1

The Basics

Learn to flip. Simple platforms, gentle gaps.

Objective:

Reach the red door at the end of the path.

Strategy:

  • • Press SPACE to flip forward
  • • Land flat on platforms
  • • Don't spam flips!
1-2

Narrow Landing

Practice precision. Narrower platforms await.

Objective:

Cross the gap using flip momentum.

Strategy:

  • • Time your flips carefully
  • • Use single flips for small gaps
  • • Double flip for longer ones
1-3

The Gap

Your first real challenge. Mind the gap!

Objective:

Clear the large chasm to proceed.

Strategy:

  • • Build momentum with 2-3 flips
  • • Rotate mid-air for alignment
  • • Commit to the jump!

2 Chapter 2: Shake It Up

2-1

Shake Introduction

Learn the shake mechanic. Build energy!

Objective:

Reach platforms using shake-enhanced jumps.

Strategy:

  • • Hold shake input to charge
  • • Release for super jump
  • • Watch the energy meter!
2-2

Vertical Climb

Go up! Use shakes to reach higher ground.

Objective:

Climb the vertical tower.

Strategy:

  • • Chain shake jumps upward
  • • Use walls for rebound
  • • Don't overcharge!
2-3

Moving Platforms

Platforms that move! Time your jumps.

Objective:

Ride moving platforms to the goal.

Strategy:

  • • Jump when platform moves close
  • • Use shake for long transfers
  • • Patience is key

3 Chapter 3: Master Flippers

3-1

The Spinner

Rotating platforms! Stay focused.

Objective:

Navigate rotating obstacles.

Strategy:

  • • Time jumps between rotations
  • • Use small flips for adjustment
  • • Watch rotation patterns
3-2

Momentum Madness

Use momentum wisely. Chain flips perfectly.

Objective:

Maintain momentum through the level.

Strategy:

  • • Chain flips without stopping
  • • Never fully land between flips
  • • Build maximum speed
3-3

Return Home

The final level. Flippy must return to his owner!

Objective:

Complete the ultimate flip challenge.

Strategy:

  • • Use everything you've learned
  • • Combine flips and shakes
  • • Trust your skills!

Co-op Walkthrough Tips

🤝

Team Flipping

Coordinate flips with teammates. One person flips, others watch for hazards.

📍

Spotter System

Assign spotters to call out platform positions and timing.

🚀

Boost Runs

Use teammates as springboards for extra height and distance.

⚡

Chain Respawns

When one player dies, others can resurrect them mid-level.
