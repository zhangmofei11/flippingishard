Tips & Tricks

Become a master flipper with these essential strategies

⚡ Core Mechanics

Flip Timing

  • Single Flip: Tap flip button once for small hops
  • Double Flip: Two quick flips for medium gaps
  • Triple Flip: Expert technique for large distances
  • Recovery: Always check your rotation before landing

Shake Mechanics

  • Quick Shake: Small energy boost, fast recharge
  • Charged Shake: Hold for 2-3 seconds for super jump
  • Overcharge: Releases automatically at max to prevent damage
  • Mid-air Shake: Use once per jump for adjustment

🎯 Precision Platforming

👁️

Focus on Landing

Always know where you want to land before flipping. Mid-air adjustment is limited.

⏱️

Wait and Watch

Moving platforms have patterns. Watch for 1-2 cycles before attempting.

🔄

Control Rotation

You can adjust rotation mid-flip. Use small inputs to fine-tune your angle.

📏

Mind the Edges

Platform edges are unforgiving. Aim for the center of your target platform.

🧘

Stay Calm

Panicking leads to missed jumps. Take a breath before tricky sequences.

🔁

Practice Death

You will die. A lot. Each death teaches you something new about the level.

🏁 Speedrun Strategies

Basic Speedrun Tech

  • • Flip Cancel: Interrupt flip animation for faster landings
  • • Wave Dash: Shake mid-flip for horizontal boost
  • • Momentum Preservation: Never fully stop between jumps
  • • Skip Clips: Some levels have clip shortcuts

Advanced Techniques

  • • Reverse Flip: Flip backward then redirect forward
  • • Shake Cancelling: Cancel shake into flip for instant speed
  • • Pixel Perfect: Frame-perfect landings for maximum efficiency
  • • Route Planning: Know your path before starting

👥 Multiplayer Co-op Tips

Team Communication

  • • Use voice chat for real-time coordination
  • • Designate a leader for callouts
  • • Call out platform timing clearly
  • • Warn teammates of hazards ahead

Advanced Co-op

  • • Stack jumps: Multiple players bouncing one player
  • • Rescue chains: Spread out for quick respawns
  • • Sacrifice plays: Some paths need a distraction
  • • Practice mode: Learn each other's timing

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🤕

Over-flipping

Less is more. Don't spam flips!

⚡

Overcharging

Max shake auto-releases. Time it!

🏃

No Planning

Know your route before jumping!

😤

Giving Up

Persistence beats talent every time.
