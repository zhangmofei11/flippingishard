The Unique Flipping Mechanic

🔄

Flip & Rotate

Master the art of controlled flipping. Flippy rotates through the air with realistic physics - time your flips perfectly to land on narrow platforms.

📳

Shake & Jump

Use shake gestures or jump commands to build momentum. The more Flippy shakes, the more energy he builds for bigger jumps!

🎯

Precision Landing

Every flip counts. Land precisely on platforms - one wrong move and Flippy tumbles into the void. Patience is key!
