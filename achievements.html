Achievement Guide

Complete challenges and unlock all achievements

📖 Story Achievements

🏠

Homeward Bound

Story

Complete the game and return Flippy to his owner.

How to unlock:

Finish all 9 main levels. Keep flipping forward!

📱

First Flip

Story

Perform your first flip as Flippy.

How to unlock:

Press flip button on Level 1-1. Automatic!

🤝

Together We Flip

Story

Complete a level in co-op mode.

How to unlock:

Play any level with 2+ players. Join or host!

💀

Determination

Story

Die 100 times. Persistence is key!

How to unlock:

Just keep playing. Death is part of the journey.

⚡ Skill Achievements

🔄

Triple Flip

Perform three consecutive flips.

Press flip rapidly 3 times mid-air

📳

Shake Master

Perfectly charge a shake jump to max power.

Hold shake for exactly 2.5-3 seconds

🎯

Pixel Perfect

Land on a platform with less than 10% overhang.

Requires extreme precision timing

⏱️

Speed Demon

Complete any level in under 30 seconds.

Chapter 1 levels are best for this

🛡️

Untouchable

Complete a level without taking any damage.

Full health run through any level

🌟

Flip Legend

Perform 50 flip jumps in a single level.

Go wild with flipping!

🏆 Challenge Achievements

🥇

World's Best Flipper

Hard

Complete the game without dying.

Difficulty:

Extremely hard. One mistake costs everything. Save often!

👥

Full House

Hard

Complete a level with all 16 players.

Difficulty:

Organize a full lobby. Chaos level: Maximum!

⚡

Lightning Fast

Hard

Complete the game in under 10 minutes.

Difficulty:

Master all levels first. Speedrun route essential.

💀

Suffer King

Hard

Die 1000 times total across all saves.

Difficulty:

Just keep dying. Embrace the suffering!

🤫 Secret Achievements

🐴

Elegant Discovery

Secret

Find the secret horse room.

How to unlock:

Hidden in Level 3-2. Wall clip behind the third rotating platform.

🎵

Beat Master

Secret

Land 10 flips perfectly on the beat.

How to unlock:

Music levels have a beat. Sync your flips to it!

🚀

Rocket Flippy

Secret

Reach maximum velocity during a flip.

How to unlock:

Chain flips perfectly for 10+ seconds. Max speed unlocked!

🌈

Flippy Pride

Secret

Complete the game with all flips rainbow-colored.

How to unlock:

Unlock all color themes first, then complete game.

Achievement Hunting Tips

📅

Plan Ahead

Some achievements require specific runs. Don't mix speedrun and no-death attempts!

🎮

Practice Levels

Use Chapter 1 to practice mechanics before attempting hard achievements.

🤝

Find Friends

Some achievements need multiplayer. Reach out to the community!
